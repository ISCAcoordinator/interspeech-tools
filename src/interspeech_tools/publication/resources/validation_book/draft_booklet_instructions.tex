\subsection{Your name appears multiple times in the index}

If you realize your name appears multiple times, this is likely due to the fact that different emails or IDs have been used to identify you.
In this case only, please send an email to \url{publication@interspeech2025.org} indicating, your name, email and the list of paper IDs that you authored.
